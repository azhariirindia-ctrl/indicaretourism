🔬 Factors That Affect IVF Success

  • Age — the single biggest factor. Success rates decline significantly after 38.
  • Diagnosis — PCOS, endometriosis, male factor infertility all have different success profiles
  • Number of cycles — cumulative success rate after 3 cycles is significantly higher than a single cycle
  • Embryo quality — clinics with advanced embryology labs (time-lapse, blastocyst culture) show better outcomes
  • Clinic experience — choose a clinic that treats 200+ IVF cycles per year

How the IVF Process Works in India

01

Pre-Travel Teleconsultation

Before you travel, indiCare arranges a video consultation with your assigned fertility specialist. Your hormone tests and medical history are reviewed. A personalised treatment plan is prepared.

02

Ovarian Stimulation (Days 1–12)

You begin hormone injections to stimulate your ovaries to produce multiple eggs. The clinic monitors your response through ultrasound scans and blood tests every 2–3 days.

03

Egg Retrieval (Day 12–14)

A short procedure under sedation, typically taking 20–30 minutes. Eggs are collected from your ovaries using a fine needle guided by ultrasound. You rest for a few hours and return to your hotel.

04

Fertilisation in the Lab (Days 14–19)

Eggs are fertilised with sperm in the embryology laboratory. The resulting embryos are monitored for quality over 3–5 days. The best quality embryo is selected for transfer.

05

Embryo Transfer (Day 19–21)

The selected embryo is placed in the uterus in a quick, painless procedure. No anaesthesia is required. You rest for a short period and then return to your accommodation.

06

Pregnancy Test & Follow-Up

A blood test 14 days after transfer confirms whether implantation was successful. If positive, your first scan is scheduled. If not, remaining frozen embryos can be transferred in a future cycle.

How Long Do I Need to Stay in India for IVF?

A standard IVF cycle requires approximately 3–4 weeks in India. Here is a typical timeline:

📅 Typical IVF Stay Timeline

  • Days 1–3: Arrival, initial consultation, baseline blood tests and scan
  • Days 4–14: Stimulation injections with clinic monitoring every 2–3 days
  • Day 14–15: Egg retrieval procedure
  • Days 14–19: Embryo culture in laboratory
  • Day 19–21: Embryo transfer
  • Days 21–35: Rest period before pregnancy blood test

With teleconsultation, some of the early monitoring can be done in your home country, reducing your stay to as little as 2 weeks.
