Heart Valve Replacement in India 2026 — Cost, Best Hospitals & Complete Guide for International Patients

Heart valve replacement in India from $6,000 — up to 90% less than USA or UK — with mechanical, biological and minimally invasive TAVI options at world-class cardiac centres.

By indiCare Team  ·  August 18, 2026  ·  8 min read

Heart valve disease affects millions of people worldwide. Whether caused by rheumatic fever — particularly common in Nigeria and South Asia — congenital defects, or age-related degeneration, damaged heart valves can be life-threatening without surgical intervention. India's world-class cardiac centres offer all forms of valve replacement at a fraction of Western costs, with outcomes that match the best hospitals globally.

India performs over 200,000 cardiac surgeries annually — more than almost any other country — and its cardiac surgeons are among the most experienced in the world. For patients from Nigeria, UAE, Kenya, and Bangladesh where rheumatic heart disease is prevalent, India represents the most accessible path to life-saving cardiac care.

Types of Heart Valve Replacement Available in India

Most Durable

Mechanical Valve Replacement

Carbon or titanium valve lasts a lifetime. Requires lifelong warfarin blood thinning medication.

  • Best for patients under 60
  • Single surgery for life
  • Requires regular INR monitoring
  • Slightly higher clicking sound
No Blood Thinners

Biological Valve Replacement

Tissue valve from pig, cow or human donor. No blood thinners needed after 3 months. Lasts 15-20 years.

  • Best for patients over 65
  • No lifelong anticoagulation
  • May need replacement after 15-20 years
  • More natural feel
Minimally Invasive

TAVI — Keyhole Valve Replacement

New valve inserted via catheter through groin — no open-heart surgery. For high-risk patients who cannot tolerate traditional surgery.

  • No open-heart surgery
  • 2-3 day hospital stay
  • Faster recovery
  • Best for elderly or high-risk patients
Valve Preserving

Mitral Valve Repair

Where possible, the damaged valve is repaired rather than replaced. Better long-term outcomes than replacement for suitable patients.

  • Preserves natural valve
  • No prosthetic valve needed
  • Better heart function outcomes
  • Available at specialist centres

💡 Rheumatic Heart Disease — A Special Note for Nigerian and South Asian Patients

Rheumatic heart disease — caused by untreated streptococcal throat infections in childhood — is the most common cause of valve disease in Nigeria, Kenya, Bangladesh and parts of the Middle East. India's cardiac surgeons have unmatched experience with rheumatic valve disease, performing thousands of these procedures every year. For patients with this diagnosis, India is the single best destination in the world for valve surgery.
