How to Get a Medical Visa for India from Nigeria — Complete 2026 Guide

Documents, costs, processing time, e-visa option, and how indiCare arranges your hospital invitation letter — completely free.

By indiCare Team  ·  August 16, 2026  ·  7 min read

Every year, thousands of Nigerian patients travel to India for heart surgery, cancer treatment, kidney transplants, IVF, and more. One of the most common questions we receive is: "How do I get a medical visa for India from Nigeria?" This guide answers everything — step by step.

India offers two routes for Nigerian patients seeking medical treatment: the standard Medical Visa applied at the Indian High Commission in Abuja or the Indian Consulate in Lagos, and the faster e-Medical Visa applied online. Both are straightforward when you have the right documents — and indiCare handles the most important one: the official hospital invitation letter.

✅ Good News for Nigerian Patients

Nigeria is on India's e-visa eligible country list. This means you can apply for an e-Medical Visa online from home, receive approval within 72 hours, and travel without visiting any embassy in person. indiCare arranges the hospital letter you need to complete this application — at no cost to you.

Two Types of Indian Medical Visa

Recommended

e-Medical Visa (Online)

  • Apply at indianvisaonline.gov.in
  • Processed in 72 hours
  • Valid for 60 days, triple entry
  • No embassy visit required
  • Fee: approximately $25 USD
  • Best for planned treatments
Standard

Medical Visa (Embassy)

  • Apply at Indian High Commission Abuja or Consulate Lagos
  • Processed in 5–7 working days
  • Valid for 1 year, multiple entry
  • Better for long-term treatments (cancer, transplant)
  • Fee: approximately $100 USD
  • Best for extended stays
Indian High Commission, Lagos — One of two locations in Nigeria where medical visas can be applied for in person

Step-by-Step: How to Apply

1

Contact indiCare — Get Your Hospital Match

Share your medical reports with indiCare via WhatsApp or email. Within 24 hours we match you to the right hospital and specialist for your condition. This is completely free.

2

Receive Your Hospital Invitation Letter

Once your hospital appointment is confirmed, indiCare arranges the official invitation letter on hospital letterhead. This is the most important document for your visa application — and we handle it entirely.

3

Apply for e-Medical Visa Online

  • Go to indianvisaonline.gov.in
  • Select "e-Medical Visa" as the visa type
  • Fill the application form — takes about 20 minutes
  • Upload your passport scan, photograph, and hospital letter
  • Pay the fee online (approximately $25 USD)
  • Submit and wait for approval — usually within 72 hours
4

Receive ETA — Download and Print

Your Electronic Travel Authorisation (ETA) is sent to your email. Print two copies and carry them with your passport. You do NOT need to visit any embassy — the e-visa is valid at all major Indian international airports.

5

Travel to India — indiCare Meets You

Your indiCare coordinator arranges airport pickup, hotel transfer, and accompanies you to your first hospital appointment. You are never alone from the moment you land.

Can My Family Travel With Me?

Yes. India allows up to 2 medical attendants to accompany a patient on a Medical Attendant Visa. This visa is applied simultaneously with the patient's Medical Visa and requires proof of relationship — a marriage certificate for a spouse, or a birth certificate for a child or parent.

The Medical Attendant Visa is valid for the same duration as the patient's visa and allows your family member to stay with you throughout your treatment. indiCare coordinates accommodation for patients and their attendants near the treating hospital.

Common Reasons Visas Are Delayed or Rejected

⚠️ Avoid These Mistakes

  • Missing hospital invitation letter — the most common reason for rejection. Always get this from indiCare before applying.
  • Passport validity under 6 months — renew your passport before applying
  • Incorrect photograph format — must be white background, no glasses, full face visible
  • Insufficient bank statement funds — show at least the estimated cost of your treatment plus $2,000
  • Applying too late — apply at least 2 weeks before your planned travel date
  • Medical records not translated — all documents should be in English
