💸 Why Are Indian Dental Costs So Low?

The cost difference is not a quality difference — it reflects lower operating costs, lower labour costs, and government subsidies on medical infrastructure in India. The implant brands, the sterilisation standards, and the clinical protocols are identical. The difference is the overhead, not the dentist.

How to Plan Your Dental Trip to India

Sending your dental X-rays and OPG scan to the clinic before travel allows the dentist to prepare a detailed treatment plan and cost estimate in advance.

A dental trip to India is not complicated, but it does require some advance planning — particularly for implant patients who will need two visits. Here is the standard process indiCare uses with all dental patients:

Step 1 — Share your records. Send your most recent dental X-rays, OPG (panoramic) scan, and photos of your teeth to indiCare. If you have a dentist's report or treatment plan from home, include that too. This allows the Indian clinic to prepare a detailed cost estimate before you book any travel.

Step 2 — Receive your quote and plan. Within 24–48 hours, indiCare will send you a confirmed cost estimate from the matched clinic, a suggested treatment timeline, and a recommendation on whether your case requires one or two visits. Straightforward veneer and crown cases are often completed in a single trip of 5–7 days. Implant patients typically need two trips.

Step 3 — Apply for your medical visa. Dental treatment qualifies for an Indian medical visa (e-MED visa). indiCare provides the hospital invitation letter required for your visa application. Processing typically takes 5–7 business days.

Step 4 — Arrive and begin treatment. Your indiCare coordinator meets you at the airport and accompanies you to your first appointment. The dentist performs a clinical examination and confirms the treatment plan. Most treatments begin within 24 hours of arrival.

💡 Implant Patients: Plan Two Trips

  • Visit 1 (5–7 days): Extraction (if needed) and implant placement. Temporary crown or healing cap fitted.
  • Wait 3–6 months at home while osseointegration occurs (implant fuses with the jawbone).
  • Visit 2 (4–6 days): Final crown or full arch prosthetic fitted. Bite adjusted. Case completed.
  • Same-day implants available for selected single-tooth cases where bone density is confirmed to be adequate.

Is Dental Treatment in India Safe?

This is the question every patient asks — and it deserves a direct answer. Yes, dental treatment at India's accredited clinics is safe. Here is what to look for:

Implant brands matter. Insist on Nobel Biocare, Straumann, Osstem, or Zimmer Biomet implants — all of which are available at India's better clinics. Avoid clinics that cannot tell you the brand of implant they are using, or who quote suspiciously low prices (under $250 per implant) — this typically means unbranded or low-quality implant systems.

Sterilisation standards. Ask whether the clinic uses an autoclave for all instruments and whether single-use items are opened in front of you. Reputable clinics will not hesitate to show you their sterilisation protocols.

Dentist qualifications. Many of India's senior dental specialists hold postgraduate degrees from UK, US, or European dental schools in addition to their Indian BDS and MDS qualifications. Ask for your dentist's CV — good clinics provide this readily.

NABH accreditation. The National Accreditation Board for Hospitals applies to dental clinics as well. NABH-accredited dental centres have been independently audited for infection control, equipment standards, and patient safety protocols.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much do dental implants cost in India?

Dental implants in India cost between $500 and $800 per implant at accredited dental clinics — compared to $3,000–$5,000 per implant in the USA or £2,000–£3,500 in the UK. Full mouth All-on-4 implants in India cost $4,000–$8,000 versus $20,000–$40,000 in the USA.

How much do veneers cost in India?

Dental veneers in India cost $150–$300 per tooth — compared to $1,000–$2,500 per tooth in the USA. A full set of 8–10 veneers costs $1,500–$3,000 in India versus $10,000–$25,000 in the USA.

How long do I need to stay in India for dental implants?

Most implant cases require two trips: the first visit (5–7 days) for implant placement, and the second visit (4–6 days) 3–6 months later for the final crown. Single-tooth cases with sufficient bone density may be eligible for same-day implants, completing the procedure in a single visit.

Do I need a medical visa for dental treatment in India?

Yes. Dental treatment qualifies for the Indian e-MED (medical) visa. You will need a hospital invitation letter — which indiCare provides — plus your passport, dental treatment plan, and proof of funds. Processing typically takes 5–7 business days online.

Can I combine dental treatment with other medical treatment in India?

Absolutely. Many indiCare patients combine dental work with orthopaedic surgery, cardiac check-ups, or oncology treatment to make the most of a single trip. Our coordinators help schedule treatments across specialties efficiently.

Does indiCare charge a fee for dental facilitation?

No. indiCare's services are completely free of charge to the patient. We are paid by our hospital and clinic partners. You pay only the clinic directly — no agency fees, no hidden charges.
