Cancer Treatment in India:
CAR-T Cell Therapy & Immunotherapy

India has developed Asia's first indigenous CAR-T therapy — delivering breakthrough cancer treatment at a fraction of what it costs in the West.

By indiCare Team  ·  August 14, 2026  ·  8 min read

A cancer diagnosis is one of the most terrifying moments a family can face. But treatment options — and outcomes — have changed dramatically. India is now home to some of the world's most advanced oncology centres, offering immunotherapy, targeted therapy, and the world's most affordable CAR-T cell therapy.

For patients who cannot access or afford treatment in the United States, United Kingdom, or Europe, India's cancer care ecosystem represents a genuine, medically equivalent alternative. This guide explains what is available, which hospitals offer it, and how indiCare helps you navigate every step.

🔬 Breakthrough: NexCAR19 — Asia's First Indigenous CAR-T Therapy

Developed jointly by ImmunoACT and IIT Bombay, NexCAR19 is India's first homegrown CAR-T cell therapy, approved for B-cell lymphomas and leukaemias. It costs approximately $40,000–$50,000 in India — compared to $400,000–$500,000 in the United States for similar therapies. Same science. Fraction of the price.

Tata Memorial Hospital, Mumbai — Asia's largest dedicated cancer hospital, treating over 70,000 new patients annually

Cancer Treatments Available in India

Breakthrough

CAR-T Cell Therapy

Chimeric Antigen Receptor T-cell therapy reprograms a patient's own immune cells to target and destroy cancer cells. NexCAR19, developed in India, is approved for relapsed or refractory B-cell cancers.

From $40,000 in India
Immunotherapy

Checkpoint Inhibitors

Drugs like pembrolizumab (Keytruda) and nivolumab (Opdivo) block proteins that prevent immune cells from attacking tumours. India offers branded and generic versions at significantly reduced costs.

60–80% cheaper than USA
Precision Medicine

Targeted Therapy

Genomic profiling and targeted drugs (EGFR inhibitors, ALK inhibitors, HER2 therapies) are widely available across major Indian oncology centres, guided by next-generation sequencing.

Full NGS from $800
Radiation

Proton Therapy & IMRT

Advanced radiation techniques including Intensity-Modulated Radiation Therapy (IMRT) and proton beam therapy are available at facilities like Apollo Proton Cancer Centre in Chennai.

Proton from $20,000
Surgery

Robotic & Minimally Invasive

Da Vinci robotic surgery is available for prostate, gynaecological, and gastrointestinal cancers, enabling faster recovery, less blood loss, and smaller incisions.

70% cheaper than USA
Blood Cancer

Bone Marrow Transplant

Both autologous and allogeneic stem cell transplants are performed at major centres in Mumbai, Delhi, Chennai, and Hyderabad, with outcomes comparable to Western transplant units.

From $25,000
"The availability of CAR-T therapy in India — at a tenth of the Western cost — is not just an economic development. It is a humanitarian one. It means patients who would otherwise have no options, now do." — Dr. Hasmukh Jain, Haematologist, Tata Memorial Centre
