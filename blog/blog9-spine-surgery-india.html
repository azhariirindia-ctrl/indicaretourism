Spine Procedures Available in India

Spinal Fusion (TLIF/PLIF)

Joining two or more vertebrae to eliminate painful movement. Used for spondylolisthesis, degenerative disc disease, and fractures.

India: $5,000–$10,000 | USA: $80,000–$150,000

Disc Replacement (ADR)

Artificial disc replaces damaged disc while preserving motion. Alternative to fusion for single-level disc disease.

India: $6,000–$12,000 | USA: $60,000–$100,000

Microdiscectomy

Minimally invasive removal of herniated disc material pressing on nerve roots. Fast recovery — most patients walk same day.

India: $4,000–$7,000 | USA: $30,000–$60,000

Laminectomy / Decompression

Removal of bone spurs and thickened ligaments causing spinal stenosis. Relieves leg pain, numbness, and weakness.

India: $4,000–$8,000 | USA: $40,000–$80,000

Scoliosis Correction

Correction of abnormal spinal curvature using rods and screws. Both adolescent and adult scoliosis treated.

India: $8,000–$15,000 | USA: $100,000–$200,000

Robotic Spine Surgery

Computer-guided robotic assistance for precise implant placement. Available at Apollo, Fortis and Medanta centres.

India: $7,000–$14,000 | USA: $100,000–$180,000
