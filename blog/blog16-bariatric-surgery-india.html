Bariatric Procedures Available in India

Gastric Sleeve (Most Popular)

75-80% of the stomach is removed laparoscopically, creating a banana-shaped sleeve. Restricts food intake and reduces hunger hormone ghrelin. Most popular bariatric procedure worldwide.

India: $4,000–$5,500 | USA: $15,000–$25,000

Gastric Bypass (Most Effective)

Small stomach pouch created and connected directly to small intestine, bypassing most of the stomach. Most effective for diabetes remission and long-term weight loss.

India: $5,000–$7,000 | USA: $20,000–$35,000

Mini Gastric Bypass

Simplified version of gastric bypass with single anastomosis. Shorter operation time, similar results to full bypass. Growing in popularity at Indian bariatric centres.

India: $4,500–$6,500 | USA: $18,000–$30,000

Revisional Bariatric Surgery

For patients who have had previous bariatric surgery with insufficient results or complications. India bariatric surgeons have extensive experience with complex revision cases.

India: $6,000–$9,000 | USA: $25,000–$40,000

💡 Who is Eligible for Bariatric Surgery?

  • BMI 40+ — eligible for surgery without additional conditions
  • BMI 35-39 with obesity-related conditions — diabetes, hypertension, sleep apnoea, joint problems
  • BMI 30-34 with uncontrolled Type 2 diabetes — eligible for metabolic surgery
  • Age 18-65 — some centres treat carefully selected younger or older patients
  • Previous weight loss attempts — must have tried diet and exercise programmes
