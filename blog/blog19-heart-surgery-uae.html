Procedures Available

01

Coronary Artery Bypass Grafting (CABG)

Most common cardiac surgery; India performs 50,000+ bypass surgeries per year with outcomes matching leading centres in the UK and USA.

02

Heart Valve Replacement & Repair

Mechanical and biological valve options available. TAVR (transcatheter) also available for high-risk surgical patients at select centres.

03

Angioplasty & Stenting

Minimally invasive with same-day discharge in many cases. Drug-eluting stents from leading international brands used.

04

Congenital & Paediatric Cardiac Surgery

Specialised centres in Delhi NCR and Chennai handle complex congenital heart defect corrections for children and adults.

India's top cardiac centres perform more than 50,000 open heart surgeries per year — a volume that drives expertise, lowers complication rates, and keeps costs significantly below Western and Gulf alternatives.— indiCare Medical Team

Dubai to Delhi: Logistics

Direct flights from Dubai to Delhi run multiple times daily — flight time approximately 3 hours. From Abu Dhabi and Sharjah, connections are similarly frequent. indiCare arranges airport pickup, hospital coordination, accommodation near the hospital, and 24/7 support in English and Arabic. India Medical Visa for UAE patients is typically approved within 3–7 business days.
