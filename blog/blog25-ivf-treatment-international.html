Treatments Available

01

Standard IVF & ICSI

ICSI (Intracytoplasmic Sperm Injection) is recommended for male factor infertility — available at all partner clinics.

02

Donor Egg IVF

For women with poor ovarian reserve or over 40. India has an active, regulated egg donor programme with high success rates.

03

Frozen Embryo Transfer (FET)

Create and freeze embryos in India, return for transfer. Reduces time away from home significantly.

04

Preimplantation Genetic Testing (PGT)

Test embryos for chromosomal abnormalities before transfer — improves success rates and reduces miscarriage risk.

How Long Do You Need to Stay?

A full IVF cycle requires 3–4 weeks in India. However, many international patients do the stimulation phase at home with their local doctor, then fly to India only for egg retrieval and embryo transfer — reducing their stay to just 10–12 days. indiCare coordinates between your home doctor and the Indian fertility clinic.
