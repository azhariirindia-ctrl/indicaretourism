🇰🇪 Kenya → India Kenyan patients choose India for cardiac surgery, liver transplant, cancer treatment, and orthopaedic surgery. Indian e-Medical Visa from Nairobi takes 3–5 days. 🇦🇪 UAE → India Patients from Dubai and Abu Dhabi travel to India for IVF, complex cardiac surgery, cancer treatment, and spine surgery at a fraction of UAE private hospital costs. Medical visa for UAE patients is straightforward. IVF from $2,000 per cycle. indiCare serves patients from across the UAE.

Flight to Delhi: ~6.5 hours, no stopover. Heart surgery $5,500–$10,000 vs $40,000–$60,000 in South Africa. East African patient community guesthouses near Fortis, Medanta, and Apollo in Delhi NCR. Top procedures: cardiac surgery, cancer treatment, kidney transplant, spine surgery. Indian e-Medical Visa processed in 24–72 hours. indiCare supports Tanzanian patients end to end.
